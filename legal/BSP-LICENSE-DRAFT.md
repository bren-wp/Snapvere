## Proposed terms

1. A non-exclusive license to install and use an official Free or Pro distribution for lawful personal, educational and internal business purposes, subject to the applicable edition entitlement. Free local operation requires no account, watermark or subscription. Pro terms must disclose duration, devices, activation, recovery and payment conditions before purchase.
2. Except with prior written permission, or where independent licenses or mandatory law permit, no redistribution of proprietary binaries or source, resale, publication of modified editions, distribution of derivative products, rebranding, white-label distribution, removal of notices, false attribution, or unauthorized use of protected marks/assets. Internal authorized business use and ordinary use of user-created output are not prohibited. Separate written agreements may authorize commercial redistribution.
3. No circumvention of entitlement or reverse engineering except where permitted by applicable law. Preserve statutory exceptions including interoperability, observation/testing, lawful backup and mandatory consumer rights. No restriction on exercise of independent MIT or third-party rights.
4. Prior lawful MIT grants remain effective for their covered material; third-party licenses and notices remain applicable. No attempt to claim proprietary rights over material without a verified grant. In conflict, independent license rights and mandatory law prevail.
5. Users retain their rights in captures, recordings and projects, subject to third-party rights. License expiry or termination must not lock, delete, encrypt or otherwise impair access to existing user media.
6. Disclose privacy, optional sharing and activation data practices separately. Local capture does not require network access. No automatic transfer of capture content without informed authorization.
7. Liability, warranty, termination, remedies and governing-law clauses require legal review and must preserve mandatory consumer protections and jurisdiction rights. Provide reasonable cure where appropriate.
8. Distribution must include applicable notices, terms and publisher identity; changes apply prospectively to covered versions, not as retroactive withdrawal of prior grants.
