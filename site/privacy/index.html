Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

The short version

  • The Snapvere app collects nothing. No telemetry, no analytics, no account. Your screenshots and recordings stay on your computer.
  • No web analytics or tracking cookies. No behavioral tracking, advertising pixels or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Snapvere installers are served directly from snapvere.com. The website host may process technical request data such as your IP address.

1. The Snapvere app

Everything Snapvere creates — screenshots, recordings, the gallery database, your settings and a local diagnostic log — is stored on your own device (inside Snapvere's per-user application-data directory, identified by com.brendigo.snapvere on Windows). The app sends no analytics, telemetry or usage data anywhere. The diagnostic log only leaves your computer if you decide to send it to us yourself.

Private share links (uploading a capture) are not available yet. Before that feature ships, this policy will be updated to explain exactly what is uploaded, where it is stored and for how long.

2. This website

Hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify, Inc. Like any web server, Netlify processes your IP address and basic request details (browser, time, page requested) to deliver pages and protect against abuse.

Fonts

The Inter typeface is served from this website itself. Your browser does not contact Google Fonts or any other font provider.

Website analytics

This site does not use any analytics or tracking cookies. It does not record visitor interactions, download button clicks or FAQ events, and does not load Google Analytics or other third-party trackers.

The hosting service necessarily processes ordinary HTTP connection information such as an IP address to deliver pages and installers. This is not application telemetry. External links clicked by a visitor lead to third-party sites governed by their own privacy terms.

3. Downloads

Snapvere installers are served directly from snapvere.com using the website's static hosting infrastructure. The hosting provider may process connection information such as your IP address to deliver downloads and operate security protections. The application itself does not require an account.

4. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access, correction or deletion of personal information processed by the hosting provider, and object to processing where applicable. The website does not build analytics profiles or link browsing history to Snapvere users. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority — in Croatia, the Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP), or the authority where you live.

5. Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact the publisher through the Brendigo website.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page.
